Monolithic Flexible TPU Robot Gripper

Topology-optimized compliant mechanism end-effector 3D printed in 95A Shore TPU for delicate object manipulation and adaptive envelope grasping.

Hardware & CAD Repo↗Firmware / ROS 2 Repo↗

Project Overview

Traditional rigid robotic parallel grippers struggle with irregularly contoured, delicate, or deformable objects without complex force-torque feedback arrays. This project presents a single-part compliant gripper leveraging monolithic flexure hinges printed in thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU).

By exploiting nonlinear elastic deformation, the gripper naturally passively wraps around variable-geometry items, distributing contact pressures evenly while requiring only a single linear servo drive.

Hardware Specifications

  • Manufacturing Process: FDM 3D Printing (Direct-Drive, 0.4mm nozzle)
  • Material: Polymaker PolyFlex TPU95-HF (Shore 95A Elastomer)
  • Stroke / Grasp Span: 0 mm (fully closed) to 110 mm (fully open)
  • Payload Capacity: Up to 1.8 kg (cylindrical form-factor objects)
  • Actuator: Feetech STS3215 Magnetic Bus Servo (19 kg·cm @ 7.4V)
  • Mounting Flange: ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 Robot End-Effector Standard

Flexure Geometry Analysis & Topology Optimization

Compliant mechanisms replace rigid joints and pin pivots with flexible segments. FEA simulations conducted in SolidWorks Simulation guided the rib distribution:

  • Stress Concentration Relief: Variable-thickness flexure hinges distribute maximum strain across parabolic contours rather than notched pivots, mitigating fatigue failures under continuous cycling.
  • Infill Anisotropy Control: Sliced with 100% rectilinear infill oriented at 45∘45^\circ45∘ parallel to the principal tensile trajectories to maximize inter-layer adhesion and prevent delamination under torsional loads.
  • Fatigue Testing: Test coupons withstood >500,000 deflection cycles to maximum travel without visible micro-cracking or permanent creep deformation.

Adaptive Grasping Mechanics

The finger kinematics utilize an inverted Fin-Ray compliant structure that produces passive conformation:

  • Contact Wrapping Effect: When the outer surface contacts an external barrier, the triangular truss geometry buckles inward toward the object rather than repelling away, drastically enhancing grasp stability.
  • Passive Force Equilibrium: Eliminates the requirement for dedicated tactile tactile sensors when handling delicate items like soft fruit, glass vials, or thin electronic assemblies.
  • Frictional Enhancements: Directional chevron micro-ridges printed directly onto the grasping contact faces double dry surface friction coefficients against polished acrylic and metal surfaces.

End-Effector Mounting Interfaces

The gripper transitions from flexible elastomeric fingers to rigid mounting hardware:

  • ISO 9409-1 Standard Flange: Base adapter machined from 6061 aluminum or printed in rigid carbon-PETG, fastening directly onto collaborative robotic arms (AgileX Piper, Universal Robots UR5e, Franka Emika Panda).
  • Quick-Change Mechanical Lock: Quarter-turn spring detent release mechanism enables finger swap operations in under 30 seconds for differing payload form factors.
  • Integrated Tendon Routing: Low-friction PTFE liner tubes channel Dyneema actuation cords straight to the servo drive pulley with negligible parasitic friction losses.
