SO-101 Robot Arm: Low-Cost Teleoperation & SmolVLA Policy Deployment

Dual 6-DOF 3D-printed leader-follower arms, Feetech bus servos, LeRobot teleoperation demonstration recording, and autonomous closed-loop SmolVLA policy execution.

Hardware & CAD Repo↗Firmware / ROS 2 Repo↗

1. Executive Summary

Engineered and deployed an end-to-end, ultra-low-cost robotics stack for the SO-101 6-DOF robotic arm, combining 3D-printed hardware, low-latency leader-follower teleoperation via Hugging Face LeRobot, and real-world physical deployment of SmolVLA (Vision-Language-Action) foundation policies.

To bypass the prohibitive multi-thousand-dollar barrier of industrial manipulators, I built paired 6-DOF leader and follower units utilizing high-torque serial bus smart servos with absolute magnetic encoder feedback. Using the 6-DOF leader arm equipped with an ergonomic kinesthetic teaching handle, I captured high-quality demonstration datasets of contact-rich manipulation tasks.

Finally, I trained and deployed a SmolVLA vision-language-action policy directly onto the physical SO-101 follower arm, achieving autonomous closed-loop pick-and-place execution with zero human intervention.

System Specifications

  • Manipulator Topology: SO-101 6-DOF Articulated Arm
  • Actuation & Bus: Feetech Smart Bus Servos (Half-duplex UART @ 1 Mbps)
  • Sensing Feedback: 12-bit Absolute Magnetic Encoders + Real-time Current/Temp
  • Teleop Configuration: 6-DOF Leader Arm with Ergonomic Pistol Grip & Trigger
  • Follower End-Effector: 2-Finger Parallel Jaw Gripper with Rubberized Pads
  • AI Policy: SmolVLA (Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model)
  • Software Stack: Hugging Face LeRobot, PyTorch, Linux serial /dev/ttyUSB
  • Total Bill of Materials: <$250 per arm (>90% cost reduction vs. commercial arms)

2. 3D-Printed Leader & Follower Hardware Architecture

To enable affordable, widespread imitation learning data collection, I manufactured and assembled multiple SO-101 arm variants featuring specialized end-effectors:

Hardware Production Array: Left: Dual 6-DOF leader arms with ergonomic kinesthetic teaching pistol grips and trigger controls. Right: Dual 6-DOF follower arms with parallel jaw grippers.

Mechanical & Ergonomic Design Highlights:

  • Ergonomic Kinesthetic Leader Grips: The leader arms feature custom-designed pistol-grip handles contoured for natural hand posture during extensive 2-hour demonstration collection sessions. An integrated trigger lever with spring return maps operator finger squeeze directly to follower gripper width.
  • High-Infill Structural Print Quality: Structural links are printed in high-toughness PETG with calibrated print orientations (45° shell trajectories) to eliminate layer separation under peak torsional joint loads.
  • Pre-Loaded Ball Bearing Hubs: Dual ball bearings support each revolute joint horn, decoupling radial shaft stresses from internal servo gearboxes to prevent premature gear tooth stripping.
  • Internal Wiring Channels: Cable channels guide servo wiring along the neutral bending axis of each link, preventing wire fatigue and pinch hazards during full-range articulation.

3. Key Engineering Milestones

Milestone 1: Hardware Fabrication & Bus Calibration

  • Servo Bus Optimization: Configured smart bus servos over half-duplex UART at 1,000,000 baud (1 Mbps), tuning servo compliance margins and punch values to eliminate static deadband without inducing oscillation.
  • Backlash & Friction Compensation: Calibrated mechanical zero-offsets across all 6 joints using absolute magnetic encoders, ensuring accurate angular correspondence between leader and follower arms.

Milestone 2: Low-Latency Teleoperation & Dataset Engineering

  • LeRobot Integration: Interfaced the leader-follower pair with Hugging Face LeRobot, streaming 60 Hz joint angles from the leader to the follower with sub-15ms physical latency.
  • Multimodal Demonstration Collection: Recorded synchronized demonstration episodes capturing dual-view RGB video (wrist and workspace cameras) alongside 6-DOF joint positions, velocities, and gripper states.
  • Data Curation: Filtered and audited trajectory data to eliminate erratic operator movements, establishing smooth, consistent demonstrations required for high-yield policy learning.

Milestone 3: SmolVLA Model Training & Edge Deployment

  • Model Training: Trained the compact SmolVLA (Vision-Language-Action) foundation model on collected demonstration episodes, optimizing loss convergence across multimodal action prediction heads.
  • Real-Time Closed-Loop Inference: Deployed the trained model directly on physical hardware. Evaluated policy reactivity against target object position shifts and visual perturbations, demonstrating successful grasp, elevation, and transfer into the sorting container.
