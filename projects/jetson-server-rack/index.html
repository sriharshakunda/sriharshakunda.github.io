Autonomous Edge Jetson Server Rack Enclosure

Custom thermal, structural, and power distribution 4U mobile rack enclosure housing clustered NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin nodes for distributed robotics compute.

Hardware & CAD Repo↗Firmware / ROS 2 Repo↗

Project Overview

Field-deployed robotics platforms running real-time point-cloud fusion and distributed multi-modal transformer policies demand substantial compute density. Standard server equipment cannot survive mobile chassis vibration, dirt ingress, or fluctuating DC battery busses.

This project covers the turnkey thermal modeling, vibration isolation, sheet metal CAD, and power distribution engineering of a compact 4U ruggedized rack enclosure accommodating three NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB developer modules and 10GbE networking infrastructure.

Hardware Specifications

  • Form Factor: Custom 4U 19-inch Half-Depth (350mm) Aluminum Chassis
  • Compute Density: 3x NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB (825 TOPS total INT8)
  • Input Power: 18V – 36V Wide-Range DC Bus (4S/6S LiFePO4 / Mobile Base)
  • Power Regulators: High-efficiency synch buck DC-DC converters (19V @ 15A per node)
  • Thermal Capacity: Forced positive-pressure tunnel rated for 250W continuous dissipation
  • Vibration Rating: MIL-STD-810G compliant elastomeric wire-rope shock isolation

Airflow Dynamics & Thermal Modeling

Managing concentrated thermal dissipation from multiple 60W+ SoCs in a confined enclosure necessitated computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulation in SolidWorks Flow Simulation:

  • Positive Pressure Chamber: Dual Noctua NF-A12x25 high-static pressure fans pull air through front-facing removable HEPA filtration cartridges, preventing ambient particulate buildup across cooling heatsinks.
  • Diverter Ducts: 3D-printed ducting shrouds channel concentrated laminar airflow directly across the extruded fin arrays of each Jetson module, maintaining SoC temperatures below 68∘C68^\circ\text{C}68∘C under prolonged 100%100\%100% synthetic matrix multiplication burn-in tests at 35∘C35^\circ\text{C}35∘C ambient.
  • Acoustic Dampening: Baffled exhaust ports lower broadband fan noise emissions below 42 dBA during nominal teleoperation sessions.

Isolated DC-DC Power Converter Rail

Mobile robot lithium battery banks undergo drastic voltage swings under sudden motor surge draws. The electrical subsystem guarantees clean, uninterruptible power delivery:

  • Wide-Input Synchronous Buck Architecture: Custom power plane PCB integrates isolated Vicor DC-DC buck regulators with over-voltage, reverse-polarity, and inrush-limiting hot-swap controllers.
  • Telemetry Monitoring: An onboard STM32 micro-BMC samples voltage, shunt current, and thermal telemetry across each channel via I2C, streaming metrics over CAN to the master robot supervisor.
  • Graceful Shutdown Battery Backup: Supercapacitor holdup circuitry provides 12 seconds of power bridge reservation to safely flush disk writes and park file systems in the event of upstream emergency power cutoff.

Dampening Sleds & Modular Cable Harnessing

Shock and vibration from mobile rough-terrain traversal can degrade board-level solder joints and high-speed PCIe connections:

  • Wire-Rope Isolators: Each Jetson sled rides on 4 tuned stainless-steel helical wire-rope isolators that dampen low-frequency ground oscillations from rough mobile robot chassis travel.
  • Serviceability Rails: Quick-release thumb latches allow individual node sleds to slide forward for memory card access, debug flashing, or maintenance in under 15 seconds.
  • Strain Relief Harnessing: High-flex igus chain-grade cables with custom 3D-printed TPU grommets and braided PET sleeving ensure ruggedized interconnects resilient to constant chassis movement.
