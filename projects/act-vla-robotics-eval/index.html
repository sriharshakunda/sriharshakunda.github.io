AgileX PiPER: End-to-End Sim2Real Teleoperation & VLA Imitation Learning Pipeline

Production ROS 2 C++ SocketCAN driver, 6-DOF SpaceMouse Cartesian teleop, MuJoCo digital twin, 71k-frame LeRobot v3 dataset curation, and closed-loop ACT physical deployment on AgileX PiPER.

Firmware / ROS 2 Repo↗

1. Executive Summary

Designed and deployed a cost-effective, end-to-end manipulation stack on physical hardware for the AgileX PiPER 6-DOF robotic manipulator.

Lacking an expensive ($2,000+) bilateral leader arm, I engineered a high-precision 6-DOF Cartesian teleoperation system using a consumer 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse, a low-latency ROS 2 C++ SocketCAN driver, and a MuJoCo digital twin.

Leveraging this teleoperation infrastructure, I collected and curated a production-grade 50-demonstration multimodal dataset (71k+ synchronized frames at 30 Hz across dual camera viewpoints and joint telemetry) and developed the training and physical closed-loop evaluation pipeline for Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT) policies.

System Specifications

  • Manipulator: AgileX PiPER 6-DOF Arm (1.5 kg payload)
  • End-Effector: Pika Adaptive Gripper
  • Perception: Intel RealSense D405 / RGB-D & Wrist Camera (V4L2)
  • Compute & GPU: NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti • Ubuntu Linux (PREEMPT_RT)
  • Teleop Hardware: 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse (~$150 vs. $2,000+ leader arm)
  • Bus Communication: Linux SocketCAN @ 1,000,000 baud (1 Mbps)
  • Control Rate: 100 Hz CAN streaming (Near-zero latency)

2. Key Engineering Milestones

Milestone 1: Production ROS 2 C++ SocketCAN Driver

  • Direct CAN Bus Communication: Implemented a multi-threaded C++ ROS 2 node communicating directly over Linux SocketCAN at 1 Mbps (1,000,000 baud) at a deterministic 100 Hz cycle time.
  • Hardware Protection & Auto-Homing: Integrated an active graceful-shutdown handler that intercepts termination signals (SIGINT / Ctrl+C) and executes a smooth minimum-jerk trajectory back to a calibrated rest pose before disengaging motor brakes, completely eliminating mechanical drops.
  • URDF & Kinematics Integration: Built comprehensive URDF/Xacro models with detailed collision/visual meshes for the arm and Pika gripper with live RViz2 visualization mirroring physical states in real time.

Milestone 2: Low-Cost 6-DOF Cartesian SpaceMouse Teleoperation

  • Zero-Dependency Input Pipeline: Engineered a direct Linux /dev/input evdev parser converting raw 6-axis analog sensor readings into normalized linear velocity (ΔX,ΔY,ΔZ\Delta X, \Delta Y, \Delta ZΔX,ΔY,ΔZ) and tool-frame angular velocity (Roll,Pitch,Yaw\text{Roll}, \text{Pitch}, \text{Yaw}Roll,Pitch,Yaw).
  • Damped Least-Squares (DLS) Differential IK: Embedded a real-time numerical Jacobian solver in MuJoCo, avoiding kinematic singularities and decoupling end-effector Cartesian motion from joint-space constraints.
  • Actuator S-Curve & Velocity Filtering: Solved the physical motor chatter problem caused by discrete Cartesian setpoints by building a 2nd-order critically-damped motion profile with hard limits (≤2.5 rad/s\le 2.5\text{ rad/s}≤2.5 rad/s velocity, ≤15.0 rad/s2\le 15.0\text{ rad/s}^2≤15.0 rad/s2 acceleration), guaranteeing C1C^1C1 velocity continuity over the CAN bus.

Milestone 3: Multimodal Demonstration Telemetry & Dataset Engineering

  • Dual-Camera Synchronized Capture: Built a high-throughput video pipeline streaming 640×480640\times 480640×480 RGB frames at 30 FPS simultaneously from a wrist/gripper camera (USB/V4L2) and an external side view (RealSense).
  • Hugging Face LeRobot v3 Integration: Recorded rich demonstration datasets pairing synchronized multi-view video streams with physical 7-DOF joint positions and commanded actions.
  • Data Quality Audit & Curation: Audited 50 recorded demonstration episodes (~40 minutes of continuous human teleoperation; 71,643 frames). Detected and excised an anomalous collision episode (bench bump during drop-off) using custom PyArrow parquet slicing and re-indexing scripts without lossy video re-encoding. Achieved 100% demonstration consistency, with sustained wide-open release windows (mean: 4.87s over the bin) to eliminate receding-horizon deadlocks during policy inference.

Milestone 4: Closed-Loop ACT Deployment & Inference Optimization

  • Receding Horizon Deadlock Mitigation: Identified and resolved a classic ACT failure mode where static hover observations caused the policy to delay drops into future action steps (t>30t > 30t>30) while commanding hold (0.010 m0.010\text{ m}0.010 m) at step t=0t=0t=0.
  • Temporal Ensembling & Settle Logic: Configured real-time temporal action ensembling (coeff = 0.01) combined with lookahead chunk release and stationary dwell triggers, delivering reliable pick-and-place execution on physical hardware.
