About Me

I am a hands-on robotics engineer with a background in mechanical engineering and mechatronics, working toward becoming a full-stack robotics engineer. So far, my primary focus has been hardware integration—selecting and integrating sensors, controllers, and actuators, designing custom mechanical parts and motion systems, and bringing up sensing pipelines on physical robots.

I am drawn to generalist robotics problems that demand a close blend of hardware and software thinking—especially systems-level architecture and integration challenges for real-time applications. Whether it is bringing up low-latency control loops, debugging sensor interfaces, or bridging CAD and rapid fabrication with physical robot bring-up, I love taking complex physical systems from concept all the way to reliable, real-world operation.
